Chapter 1 Blueprint

How the first five minutes grab the player, how the world is built to be played for weeks rather than watched for hours, and every beat of Chapter 1 on one tension graph.

01

The cold open: the first seven minutes

The game starts in the middle of the danger, six weeks into the story. The player runs, fights, faces a boss and pays a memory before they know who Nysa is. Then: SIX WEEKS EARLIER. The full screenplay is Scene 0 in the Chapter 1 script.

02

03

The shape of the world

Lethe isn't a corridor. Each layer is a large explorable region with a main path and several optional areas. They are all connected through a hub, and the tools you earn later open places you couldn't reach before. Players will go back.

04

Different directions: paths and choices

In every level

Three ways through

Most areas can be crossed by fighting (loud, costly), stealth (slow, safe), or climbing (hidden routes for players who look up). Each way finds different things.

In every layer

Whose side are you on?

The forgotten disagree. At Roanoke, some colonists want to stay in the fog and forget; others want to be found. Who you help decides who fights beside you later, and what you learn.

All game

Which memory to spend

Every spent memory is gone for good and changes later scenes. No two players reach Eesa remembering the same things.

As Eesa

The past changes the present

Where Eesa carves, hides or breaks something, fifteen years later Nysa finds the result. Some puzzles can only be solved by going back as him.

The end

Three endings and a secret one

Decided by the memories you kept, the names you returned and the allies you made. The secret ending needs all seven of Eesa's stars in every layer.

After the end

New Game+

Play again keeping one memory you lost. Characters notice. New lines, new secrets, one new scene.

06

Where players get stuck, and want to

Optional

Sealed Vaults

One or two per layer. Hard puzzle rooms the Sealers built to hide what they erased. The reward is lore and a rare keepsake.

Optional

Warden's Trials

Combat arenas with ranked challenges ("no damage", "Lantern only"). Beating one masters that layer's relic weapon.

Collectible

Eesa's 49 stars

Seven hidden stars in each layer, each with a line from his notebook. All 49 unlock the secret ending.

Mandatory

The Wardens

One boss per chapter. Each breaks the game's rules in its own way, so it has to be learned. Many players will die several times. That's intended.

Mandatory

Cross-time puzzles

The ones players argue about online: the answer is fifteen years in the past.

Safety net

The hint ladder

Stuck too long? Eesa's notebook gets more specific, one line at a time. Nobody quits for good.
