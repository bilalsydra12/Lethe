# LETHE · Chapter 1 · Scene List

**Working title:** *The Stars on the Ceiling*
**Covers:** the prologue (our world, 2026) and Layer 1, The Vanished (1590)
**Length:** about 60–90 minutes of play
**Status:** draft v0.1 for approval. The full novel-style script comes after this is agreed.

## PART A · New York

### Scene 1 · NOW · "The Painted-Over Child"
- **When:** Friday 5 June 2026, 6:40 pm
- **Where:** the Ellery Museum's paintings conservation studio, 5th floor. North-facing windows, late sun, a smell of solvent and coffee.
- **Weather:** 26 °C, humid, thunderstorms forecast for the night.
- **Who:** Nysa; **Dana Okafor**, senior conservator, 54, kind, reading glasses on a chain.
- **Nysa wears:** a grey work apron over a lavender T-shirt, hair clipped up with a pencil, a magnifying visor, white cotton gloves, chipped glitter nail polish showing at the cuffs. Her lavender scarf hangs on the chair.
- **What happens:** Nysa is cleaning *The Hartley Family* (1871, fictional) for the museum's summer exhibition, **"Erased: Faces History Removed"** (opens 20 June). Pinned on the board beside her are printouts for the exhibition panels: the **Severan Tondo** (the Roman family painting with Geta's face scraped out) and Stalin's photo with **Yezhov** edited away. As she removes a later layer of paint from a dark curtain, a **fifth child's face** appears underneath. Someone painted a child out of the family. She stops working, her hand shaking. Dana thinks it's excitement.
- **The call:** her phone buzzes with a WhatsApp video call from Ammi in Chandsar. They've sold the house and are moving to Islamabad. The buyer takes the keys on 20 June. Can she come and clear out "the store room"?
- **Player does:** the cleaning mini-game (slow, careful swabbing). It teaches patience and precision, the opposite of the chisel later.
- **Feeling:** wonder, then a cold recognition.
- **Plants:** painted-out faces as a theme. The store room.
- **History:** the Severan Tondo; the vanished commissar.

### Scene 2 · NOW · "Four Faces"
- **When:** same night, 11:20 pm
- **Where:** Nysa's apartment, a 4th-floor walk-up on 37th Avenue, Jackson Heights. Purple string lights, plants on the fire escape, frames she has restored leaning on every wall.
- **Weather:** thunderstorm, rain drumming on the air conditioner.
- **What happens:** she takes the family photograph out of her sketchbook, where she keeps it flat. Four faces. She stopped showing it to people years ago. On the shelf sits a Lego rocket that nobody in her life remembers buying. She books a flight: JFK to Islamabad.
- **Player does:** explores the apartment. Objects build her character: voice notes from Ammi, a sticky note reading "call Abbu, Sunday", a sketchbook full of faces.
- **Feeling:** a loneliness she has made peace with, until tonight.
- **Rule established:** when someone is erased, records and memories vanish, but **objects stay**. They become things nobody can explain, and other people's eyes slide past them.

## PART B · Home: Chandsar

### Scene 3 · NOW · "The Road Up"
- **When:** Saturday 13 June 2026, 5:30 am to 3 pm
- **Where:** Islamabad airport, then north on the N-15 through Abbottabad, Mansehra, Balakot (rebuilt after the 2005 earthquake), Kaghan and Naran, up to Chandsar.
- **Weather:** 38 °C and hazy in Islamabad, cooling every hour. By Naran, 17 °C with snow on the peaks. The Kunhar River runs grey-green and loud with snowmelt.
- **Who:** Nysa and Abbu, in his old white Corolla with prayer beads hanging from the mirror.
- **What happens:** painted trucks, roadside chai at Balakot, landslide rubble near Kaghan. Abbu talks about the move and his new university job. She asks carefully: "Do you remember the trip to Saiful Muluk?" He remembers it fondly: "The three of us. You got so sunburned."
- **Player does:** conversation choices in the car. Some unlock small details about her childhood.
- **Feeling:** the ache of being home and alone at the same time.
- **History:** the 2005 earthquake (real), mentioned in passing.

### Scene 4 · NOW · "The Store Room"
- **When:** same day, 10:05 pm
- **Where:** the family house in Chandsar: stone walls, deodar-wood beams, a green-painted tin roof, a *bukhari* wood stove in the main room, a kitchen smelling of chai and parathas.
- **Weather:** clear, 7 °C, very bright stars.
- **What happens:** Ammi hugs her too long and feeds her too much. Then the store room: trunks, rolled quilts, sacks of rice, a broken sewing machine, a cricket bat nobody can explain. It was Eesa's room. Nysa switches off the light, and **faint green glow-in-the-dark stars appear on the ceiling**. Seven of them form the **Big Dipper**. Ammi, from the doorway: "What stars, beta? Come and eat."
- **Player does:** first-person exploration with a phone torch. Switching off the light is the reveal.
- **Feeling:** the first crack in the world.
- **Plants:** the seven stars.

### Scene 5 · NOW · "Behind the Panel"
- **When:** 1:30 am
- **Where:** the store room, where Nysa has made a bed on the floor out of quilts.
- **What happens:** she can't sleep. In the glow, she notices that one deodar wall panel under the seventh star sits slightly proud of the others. Behind it is a **school exercise notebook** with a cricketer on the cover and Eesa's handwriting inside: his **dream journal from 2011**. A palisade in fog with a carved word. A black river full of pages. A sun with hands. Seven stars carefully mapped. The last page, dated **16/06/2011**, reads: *"Chandsar is a door. The dreams are real places. I'm going to find them."* On the facing page there's a drawing of a glass of water with a red moon reflected in it, and nothing else.
- **Player does:** reads the notebook page by page. A collectible system starts here.
- **Feeling:** proof that she isn't mad. And the grief, all over again.
- **Plants:** the red-moon drawing, which pays off in Part Three.

### Scene 6 · THEN · "Blood Moon"
- **When:** night of 15–16 June 2011. A **total lunar eclipse** was visible across Pakistan that night (real); the moon was fully red from about 00:20 to 02:00 local time.
- **Where:** the flat roof of the same house, then the small lake behind it, **Chandsar** itself.
- **Weather:** clear, 5 °C, no wind.
- **Who:** Eesa, 14: tall for his age, black hair too long, a hand-me-down fleece over his kameez, a cheap kids' **space watch with a rocket** on it from Naran bazaar, a plastic telescope.
- **What happens:** the moon turns red. The lake below begins to **glow faintly**, and its surface is warm to the touch, even though it's snowmelt. He fills his steel school water bottle. Downstairs he wakes Nysa, half-asleep, and makes her take a sip: *"Drink, choti. It's for remembering."* She drinks and falls back asleep.
- **Player does (as Eesa):** a telescope mini-game, finding the seven stars and noting them in the journal. Climbing down. Filling the bottle.
- **Feeling:** wonder and a little fear: a boy who knows something is happening.
- **Locked:** this is the Spring's water surfacing in Chandsar. Players don't learn that until Part Three.

### Scene 7 · THEN, as a memory · "The Night He Walked In"
- **When:** 17 June 2011, 2:15 am, the night after the eclipse
- **Where:** Nysa's childhood bedroom window, looking down at the lake.
- **Weather:** full moon, silver light, 6 °C.
- **Who:** Nysa, 10, in cartoon-print pyjamas. Eesa at the water's edge with the notebook in a plastic bag.
- **What happens:** she wakes for no reason and goes to the window. Eesa is standing at the edge of the lake. He looks back at her window and **lifts one hand**. Then he walks into the water until it closes over his head. The surface doesn't move. On the far shore a **man in a grey coat** is watching; his face is never shown. In the morning, nobody remembers there was ever a boy.
- **Player does (as Nysa, 10):** a short playable memory. You can only walk to the window and press your hand to the glass.
- **Feeling:** the defining moment of her life, played quietly.
- **Triggered:** this memory plays during Scene 8, in NOW.

### Scene 8 · NOW · "The Lake That Isn't"
- **When:** Tuesday 16 June 2026, 9 am. Fifteen years to the night. That evening a few black flags go up on rooftops in the valley. Shown, never explained.
- **Where:** behind the house. The lake was drained in 2012, and in its place is an **abandoned hotel foundation**: concrete pillars, rusted rebar, a faded sign reading *"MOONLAKE CONTINENTAL · COMING SOON"*.
- **Weather:** bright and cold, a strong wind, snow on **Malika Parbat** in the distance.
- **Who:** Nysa; **Baba Fazal**, about 80, who sells tea from a kettle on a gas burner by the road.
- **What happens:** Abbu says the lake "was always a marsh." Baba Fazal tells her the old story of **Saiful Muluk**: the prince who fell in love with a fairy princess at the lake, and how the fairies come down to bathe on full-moon nights. "Every lake up here has a door," he says, laughing. In the pit, a **single puddle reflects stars in daylight**. Near it is a stone with fresh chisel marks.
- **Player does:** explores the site. Touching the puddle triggers the memory of Scene 7.
- **Feeling:** anger. Someone did this on purpose.
- **Myth:** the Saiful Muluk legend (real folklore of the Kaghan Valley).

### Scene 9 · NOW · "The First Dream"
- **When:** that night
- **What happens:** a **playable dream**: fog, a wooden palisade, a bell that never stops, the word CROATOAN, and a boy's voice counting stars. She wakes at 4 am with the smell of woodsmoke and sea fog in her hair.
- **Feeling:** the calling has found her.

## PART C · The Watchers: New York

### Scene 10 · NOW · "Recurring"
- **When:** Monday 22 June 2026
- **Where:** her apartment, then the museum. "Erased" opened two days ago. She hasn't slept properly in a week.
- **Weather:** a 31 °C heatwave.
- **What happens:** she dreams every night and draws every dream in the morning. They match Eesa's notebook exactly. Online, she finds **Roanoke and CROATOAN**, and **laser scans of lost cities** that match shapes in her dreams. Dana, worried, gives her a card for a sleep clinic: the **Lindqvist Institute for Sleep Medicine** on East 68th Street.
- **Player does:** a research-board mini-game, pinning dream drawings next to real photographs.
- **History:** Roanoke (1590); LiDAR discoveries (2018, 2024).

### Scene 11 · NOW · "Dr. Vey"
- **When:** Wednesday 24 June, 9:00 am
- **Where:** the clinic: white walls, a soft lavender scent, a fountain in reception.
- **Who:** **Tomas Vey**, 64: silver hair, rimless glasses, a grey wool coat on the hook even in June, a soft voice with a faint European accent.
- **What happens:** he listens beautifully. He asks about names, and gently explains that the ancient Egyptians believed a person's name was part of their soul. When Nysa says the word from Eesa's notebook, *Lethe*, he smiles and tells her about the oracle of Trophonius in Greece, where visitors drank from two real springs, Lethe to forget and Mnemosyne to remember, before going down into a cave. "People have always wanted to choose what they forget," he says. To the player it sounds like history. Later it turns out to be the Sealers' own origin. He recommends an overnight sleep study: "We'll help your mind let go of what's troubling it."
- **Player does:** dialogue choices. Looking around his office, you find a small brass chisel used as a paperweight, and a notebook he closes when he sees you looking.
- **Feeling:** being understood by someone, too easily.
- **History and myth:** the Egyptian *ren*; Greek dream temples (Asclepius), echoed in the clinic.

### Scene 12 · NOW · "The Overnight"
- **When:** that night, 2:40 to 3:30 am
- **Where:** sleep room 4B.
- **What happens:** wired to sensors, she sleeps while a low tone plays through the room. She dreams the **River of Ink**, and Eesa's voice says: *"Don't drink their water."* She wakes. Her journal entries on the bedside table are **fading**. She pulls off the sensors and escapes through the clinic to get the notebook back from her locker. On the way out she passes Tomas's dark office and, on impulse, takes the **brass chisel** from his desk. It becomes her main weapon in Lethe.
- **Player does:** a stealth escape. The in-game journal visibly losing entries is the first taste of erasure.
- **Writers' note:** Tomas watches her leave on a monitor and lets her go. He wants to see where she leads. Revealed later.

### Scene 13 · NOW · "The Door in the Dream"
- **When:** Thursday 25 June, dawn
- **What happens:** her latest dream showed T-shaped stone pillars and a door. That morning, the museum's partner excavation in Turkey reports **a newly found sealed chamber** at a Taş Tepeler site and asks for a stone conservator. Nysa volunteers, and Dana signs off.

## PART D · The First Door: Turkey

### Scene 14 · NOW · "Şanlıurfa"
- **When:** Thursday 2 July 2026, evening
- **Where:** Şanlıurfa's old city: the **sacred fish pools at Balıklıgöl** at dusk, the covered bazaar, then the dig house, a whitewashed village house with camp beds and a generator.
- **Weather:** 41 °C in the day, 27 °C at night, a dry wind off the Harran plain.
- **Who:** **Dr. Elif Arslan**, excavation director, 46, sunburned, practical and funny.
- **What happens:** a quiet arrival. Nysa watches the fish in the pools. No comment, just stillness.

### Scene 15 · NOW · "Under the Dust"
- **When:** Friday 3 July, 4:30 am to 7:40 pm. Digs start before dawn because of the heat.
- **Where:** the trench, about 4 m deep, with T-shaped pillars half-exposed.
- **What happens:** at sunrise over the plain, Nysa begins cleaning the door: a limestone slab covered in **dozens of names in many scripts, all chiseled away**. Under her brush, one name remains in plain capital letters, **EESA**, with seven small stars beside it. Nobody else can see it. At dusk a car arrives. Tomas steps out in his grey coat, with papers saying he's a consultant. At the edge of the pit he says quietly: *"How can you still see his face?"* Then **the door opens from the other side**. Grey water spills out, and Nysa steps through.
- **Player does:** brush-cleaning (a callback to Scene 1), the confrontation, then a choice: answer Tomas, or just go.
- **This is the reveal trailer.**

## PART E · Lethe

### Scene 16 · "The Shoal of Names"
- **Where:** grey shallows under a white sky. Names float on the water like leaves.
- **Who:** an old woman sitting on an upturned boat, offering a bowl of soup to the newly lost: *"Drink, and it won't hurt any more."* (Inspired by Meng Po of Chinese tradition.)
- **What happens:** Nysa refuses the soup. A creature of the Grey rises. She **spends her first memory**, "The night Eesa showed me Saturn", and violet light tears through it. Her journal page about that night scratches itself out. Then she **calls Eesa's name across the water**, as a Roman general once called his soldiers across the Limia, and the water parts to show the way down.
- **Player does:** the first chisel use; the name-calling mechanic.

### Scene 17 · THEN · "Alkaid"
- **When:** 2011, Eesa's first days in Lethe.
- **Where:** the fog colony.
- **What happens:** Eesa, 14, frightened and fascinated, carves his first star into the post beside CROATOAN. **The player chooses exactly where on the post to carve it.**
- **Match cut:** the same post, fifteen years later.

### Scene 18 · NOW · "The Vanished" (Layer 1 · 1590 · 436 years ago)
- **Where:** a palisade colony in endless fog: lanterns, open doors, tables still set, a bell that never stops. Everyone who vanished without a trace lives here: colonists in 1580s wool, a lighthouse keeper still in oilskins, a woman in a 1920s coat. All of them whisper their own names so they won't forget.
- **What happens:** Nysa searches the post, exactly where the player carved the star as Eesa. **The star is fresh. The wood is still pale.** He was here. At the colony well she drops a lantern in, and it falls for a very long time.
- **End of Chapter 1.** End screen: *"Will you remember them?"*

## Logic fixes made while building this list

1. **Where the Spring's water came from.** Before, Eesa somehow had water from the bottom of Lethe before he'd ever gone there. Now **the lake at Chandsar is fed by the Spring**, and on rare nights, like the blood moon of June 2011, its water rises. That's when Eesa filled the bottle. He went in the next night.
2. **Objects of the erased.** Records and memories vanish, but objects stay, and other people's eyes slide past them. That's why the stars and the notebook were still there.
3. **Why Nysa goes to Turkey.** Her museum is the excavation's partner, and the dig asks for a stone conservator. It's her real job, so she has a real reason to be there.
4. **Why Tomas lets her escape.** He wants to see which door she leads him to. That's revealed in Part Two.
